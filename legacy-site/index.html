About Me

Hi, I'm Dev Kumar

Ever since childhood, I have had a deep passion for design and painting. During my school days, I participated in numerous competitions and brought home many first-prize trophies for my school. I formally learned painting and sketching, starting with facial portraits and smaller sketches, before transitioning this art onto hands.

With continuous dedication and hard work, I learned the art of Mehendi in 2008. My passion for painting and Mehendi only grew stronger over time. Gradually, I began winning Mehendi competitions and started training young students so that they too could become skilled artisans and earn a respectable livelihood.

As my artistic journey evolved, a thought struck me: "If I can apply Mehendi, why not create tattoos?" Driven by this idea, I pursued the art of tattooing and began taking professional tattoo classes.

In 2011, I made my mark at the national level by competing in prestigious competitions hosted by Grihshobha, Meri Saheli, Mahila Yug, and The Times of India, ultimately winning the Grihshobha National Certificate for 2011.

I continued to push my boundaries and expand my education so that this art form keeps thriving among the younger generation. Today's youth, children, and students looking for a new direction can learn Mehendi, Tattoo Making, and Nail Art. This art field is an excellent career foundation for the future—students who have completed their 10th or 12th grade can join these classes and build a successful career as skilled artists.

Over the last 18 years dedicated to the world of art, I have won many competitions and achieved a great deal. From every contest I won, I gained valuable insights, and from every contest I didn't, I learned even more.
